Case study 01 · Concept

Helping more users reinvest

Problem to explore. If only 5% of eligible users reinvest, what prevents the remaining users from doing so, and which product changes could responsibly improve the rate?

1. UnderstandReview the funnel, cohort behavior, user interviews and drop-off points.
2. DiagnoseTest hypotheses around trust, liquidity needs, awareness, timing and perceived risk.
3. PrioritizeScore opportunities by user value, business impact, confidence and effort.
4. MeasureTrack eligible-user reinvestment rate, funnel conversion, retention and complaints.

Responsible product principle: do not use dark patterns or pressure users into financial decisions. Any prompts should be transparent, timely and aligned with users’ goals and risk understanding.

Case study 02 · Concept

Make order tracking easier to trust

Opportunity. Users may repeatedly switch between screens to understand whether their food is being prepared, picked up or delivered. Could a clearer tracking experience reduce uncertainty?

User problemProgress and next steps may not feel clear at a glance.
Potential solutionA persistent timeline, clearer status messages and proactive delay updates.
ExperimentCompare the existing flow with a prototype through usability tests or an appropriately designed experiment.
Success measuresTask completion, support contacts, tracking-related exits and user satisfaction.

These are hypotheses and proposed metrics, not measured results.

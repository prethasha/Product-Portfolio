Case study 01 · Concept

Helping more users reinvest

Problem to explore. If only 5% of eligible users reinvest, what prevents the remaining users from doing so, and which product changes could responsibly improve the rate?

01 · Understand

Review the funnel, cohort behavior, user interviews and drop-off points.

02 · Diagnose

Test hypotheses around trust, liquidity needs, awareness, timing and perceived risk.

03 · Prioritize

Score opportunities by user value, business impact, confidence and effort.

04 · Measure

Track eligible-user reinvestment rate, funnel conversion, retention and complaints.

Responsible product principle: prompts should be transparent and aligned with users’ goals and risk understanding. Avoid pressure or dark patterns in financial decisions.

Case study 02 · Concept

Make order tracking easier to trust

Opportunity. Users may repeatedly switch between screens to understand whether their food is being prepared, picked up or delivered. Could a clearer tracking experience reduce uncertainty?

User problem

Progress and next steps may not feel clear at a glance.

Potential solution

A persistent timeline, clearer status messages and proactive delay updates.

Experiment

Compare the current flow with a prototype through usability tests or a suitable experiment.

Success measures

Task completion, support contacts, tracking-related exits and user satisfaction.

These are hypotheses and proposed metrics, not measured results.

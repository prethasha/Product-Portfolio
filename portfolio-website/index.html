01 · About me

From coordinating projects to owning products.

I'm a Certified Scrum Product Owner with a Computer Science engineering background and four years of experience across project delivery and business operations. At BrandStory I managed technical projects end to end — gathering client requirements, aligning developers, designers and SEO teams, and delivering on time. At Siemens I work inside order-to-cash operations, where I see first-hand how process gaps affect customers and teams.

Both roles taught me the same thing: good products start with understanding the real problem. I'm now moving into product ownership, where I can turn user and business needs into well-prioritised, valuable solutions. I'm also completing an MBA (IGNOU, Dec 2026).
